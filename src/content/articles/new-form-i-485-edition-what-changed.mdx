USCIS has updated Form I-485 (Application to Register Permanent Residence or Adjust Status) with new public benefits disclosure requirements, effective September 18, 2026. The updated form will be mandatory that same day—and filing the prior edition after the deadline will trigger automatic rejection. If you file adjustment cases or advise Green Card applicants, you need to know the changes and the hard deadline.

## What changed

A new rule takes effect September 18, 2026, the same day the updated form becomes mandatory, and asks adjustment of status applicants to disclose prior use of means-tested public benefits.

The form's public benefits section has been consolidated. The current edition asks two separate questions: Whether an applicant ever received cash assistance, such as Supplemental Security Income or Temporary Assistance for Needy Families, and if they accepted long-term institutionalization paid for by the government. In contrast, USCIS's new I-485 petition only asks whether the beneficiary ever received any means-tested public benefit.

If a Green Card applicant answers yes, they must list the benefit, the dates they received it, the dollar amount, and the reason they needed it.

## Why it matters

The mandatory edition date creates a hard filing deadline. If you submit the wrong version on or after September 18, USCIS will automatically reject your case. While that's different from a denial, which goes in your record, it can still delay your case by weeks.

The consolidated public benefits question also simplifies disclosure but broadens it—applicants now disclose any means-tested benefit received, not just the two previously asked. This affects who needs to include supplementary documentation and who may trigger a public charge review.

You have approximately four weeks to audit your forms inventory and ensure your template and filed cases track the edition date.

## Way forward

- **Verify the edition date.** Check the edition date at the bottom of the form before you file. The new version must be used on or after September 18, 2026.
- **Update your intake and advisory template.** Begin asking clients now about all means-tested benefits received (not just two). Document the amounts and dates.
- **Audit pending cases.** If you have I-485 applications pending by September 17, file them using the current edition. Do not delay filings to use the new edition.
- **Monitor USCIS form pages.** The new edition will be posted at https://www.uscis.gov/i-485. Confirm the edition number matches before downloading.
