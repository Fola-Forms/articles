CBP One is the [free mobile application](https://www.cbp.gov/about/mobile-apps-directory/cbpone) that [U.S. Customs and Border Protection](https://www.cbp.gov) deploys to channel a long list of traveler-facing interactions — commercial truck-driver inspections, [I-94 retrieval](https://www.cbp.gov/travel/international-visitors/i-94-instructions), pleasure-boat reporting under the [Small Vessel Reporting System](https://www.cbp.gov/travel/pleasure-boats-private-flyers/pleasure-boat-overview/svrs) — into a single authenticated channel. What made the app a national-policy story was the January 12, 2023 expansion that opened an [appointment-scheduling module](https://www.cbp.gov/newsroom/national-media-release/cbp-releases-january-2023-monthly-update) for noncitizens physically present in central or northern Mexico to request a port-of-entry inspection appointment at one of eight southwest border land ports. That single feature converted CBP One from a back-office tool into the de facto gateway for the only authorized pathway to present at the southern border for processing under [INA 235](https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title8-section1225).

## What changed

The January 2023 expansion built on a [pilot launched in 2020](https://www.cbp.gov/newsroom/national-media-release/cbp-launches-cbp-one-mobile-application) for trade and travel functions and a November 2021 narrow extension that allowed [vulnerable migrants identified by partner organizations](https://www.dhs.gov/news/2022/05/03/dhs-statement-end-title-42-public-health-order) to schedule humanitarian-exception appointments under the then-operative [Title 42 public-health order](https://www.cdc.gov/coronavirus/2019-ncov/cdcresponseorder.html). When [Title 42 lifted on May 11, 2023](https://www.dhs.gov/news/2023/05/10/fact-sheet-us-government-announces-sweeping-new-actions-manage-regional-migration), CBP One became the central scheduling mechanism for the parallel Title 8 framework rolled out under the [Circumvention of Lawful Pathways final rule](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2023/05/16/2023-10146/circumvention-of-lawful-pathways), 8 C.F.R. 208.33 and 1208.33, which presumes [asylum ineligibility](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2023/05/16/2023-10146/circumvention-of-lawful-pathways) for noncitizens who entered between ports of entry or transited a third country without first availing themselves of a lawful pathway — with a CBP One appointment being one of the [rebuttable exceptions](https://www.uscis.gov/sites/default/files/document/policy-manual-updates/20230511-CircumventionLawfulPathways.pdf).

Eligibility for the asylum-appointment module sits inside the app: the noncitizen must be in [central or northern Mexico](https://help.cbp.gov/s/article/Article-1942), submit identifying information including a live selfie used for [facial-comparison biometric matching](https://www.cbp.gov/travel/biometrics), select one of eight ports ([Brownsville, Eagle Pass, Hidalgo, Laredo, El Paso, Nogales, Calexico West, or San Ysidro](https://www.cbp.gov/about/mobile-apps-directory/cbpone)), and enter a daily lottery for the [approximately 1,450 appointment slots](https://www.dhs.gov/news/2023/06/30/fact-sheet-update-southwest-border-security-and-preparedness-ahead-may-11) DHS allocates across ports. Slot allocation, language availability (Spanish, English, Haitian Creole, Portuguese, and Russian were added in [stages](https://www.cbp.gov/newsroom/national-media-release/cbp-announces-additional-language-options-cbp-one-mobile-app)), and the geographic eligibility envelope are administrative settings DHS can adjust without rulemaking. The Department published the [interim use guidance](https://www.cbp.gov/document/guidance/cbp-one-mobile-application-arrival-procedures-and-appointments-southwest) describing the appointment-check-in workflow and the consequences of a missed appointment.

Once a noncitizen receives an appointment, they present at the named port at the scheduled time, undergo [INA 235 inspection](https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title8-section1225), and may be paroled into the United States under [INA 212(d)(5)(A)](https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title8-section1182) or processed for [expedited removal](https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title8-section1225) depending on intake decisions and any credible-fear claim. A parole-in grant — typically [two years with work-authorization eligibility](https://www.uscis.gov/working-in-the-united-states/information-for-employers-and-employees/employment-authorization) under [8 C.F.R. 274a.12(c)(11)](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-8/chapter-I/subchapter-B/part-274a/section-274a.12) — establishes lawful presence pending the noncitizen's subsequent affirmative asylum filing on [Form I-589](https://www.uscis.gov/i-589) within the [one-year filing deadline at INA 208(a)(2)(B)](https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title8-section1158).

The policy footing has always been fragile. The [Circumvention rule litigation](https://www.aclu.org/cases/east-bay-sanctuary-covenant-v-biden) — East Bay Sanctuary Covenant v. Biden in the Northern District of California — produced [vacatur on July 25, 2023](https://www.courtlistener.com/docket/68043538/east-bay-sanctuary-covenant-v-biden/), an immediate Ninth Circuit stay pending appeal, and continued litigation that has so far left the rule and the CBP One appointment scheme in place. State plaintiffs in [Texas v. DHS](https://www.texasattorneygeneral.gov/news/releases/attorney-general-ken-paxton-sues-biden-administration-over-massive-illegal-parole-program-end-title) have separately challenged the parole authority underlying the CBP One processing posture.

## Why it matters

For a noncitizen waiting in northern Mexico, CBP One is the only currently authorized way to present at a southwest border port of entry without falling into the [Circumvention rule's presumption of asylum ineligibility](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2023/05/16/2023-10146/circumvention-of-lawful-pathways). The presumption is rebuttable, but the rebuttal categories are narrow — imminent and extreme threat to life, medical emergency, severe form of trafficking — and require evidentiary showings most noncitizens are not positioned to make at the moment of inspection. A CBP One appointment skips the presumption entirely.

The daily-slot math also matters. With approximately 1,450 daily slots and demand orders of magnitude higher, the wait time for an appointment has stretched into [months at the most-requested ports](https://www.washingtonoffice.org/blog/cbp-one-app-wait-times). Geographic eligibility limited to central and northern Mexico means a noncitizen physically present in [southern Mexico, Central America, or further south](https://help.cbp.gov/s/article/Article-1942) cannot even register for the lottery — which has driven [internal migration through Mexico](https://www.dhs.gov/news/2023/06/30/fact-sheet-update-southwest-border-security-and-preparedness-ahead-may-11) toward the eligibility envelope.

For practitioners, the workflow has practical consequences. A CBP One appointment supports a stronger record of lawful intent at any later [credible-fear interview](https://www.uscis.gov/humanitarian/refugees-and-asylum/asylum/questions-and-answers-credible-fear-screening) and rebuts a Circumvention-rule presumption against the noncitizen at the affirmative-asylum filing stage. Missed appointments without rescheduling produce a [60-day cooldown](https://www.cbp.gov/document/guidance/cbp-one-mobile-application-arrival-procedures-and-appointments-southwest) before a new appointment can be requested and may be cited at a later removal proceeding as evidence of nonappearance.

Operationally, every CBP One case lives one administrative-action away from a different posture. A new administration's [revocation of the underlying parole policy](https://www.uscis.gov/sites/default/files/document/policy-manual-updates/20230511-CircumventionLawfulPathways.pdf), a successful [Texas v. DHS](https://www.texasattorneygeneral.gov/news/releases/attorney-general-ken-paxton-sues-biden-administration-over-massive-illegal-parole-program-end-title) ruling, or a final adverse decision in the [East Bay Sanctuary Covenant](https://www.courtlistener.com/docket/68043538/east-bay-sanctuary-covenant-v-biden/) appeal could shut the appointment channel or reverse the Circumvention rule's presumption framework. Practitioners should set client expectations accordingly.

## Way forward

If your client is in central or northern Mexico, download CBP One from the [Apple App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/cbp-one/id1422576539) or [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=gov.dhs.cbp.one), create the account at the [CBP One landing page](https://www.cbp.gov/about/mobile-apps-directory/cbpone), and enter the daily lottery for the noncitizen's port-of-preference. The selfie must match the [photo CBP captures on intake](https://www.cbp.gov/travel/biometrics) — a substantially different appearance at the port is grounds for an additional secondary inspection.

If your client receives an appointment, plan the travel to the port to clear the [inspection window comfortably](https://www.cbp.gov/document/guidance/cbp-one-mobile-application-arrival-procedures-and-appointments-southwest), bring the [I-589 documentation set](https://www.uscis.gov/i-589) and the [country-conditions evidence](https://www.uscis.gov/humanitarian/refugees-and-asylum/asylum/preparing-for-your-asylum-interview) you would expect at the affirmative interview, and prepare the client for [credible-fear screening](https://www.uscis.gov/humanitarian/refugees-and-asylum/asylum/questions-and-answers-credible-fear-screening) if a fear-of-return claim is raised at intake. Parole grants are not asylum and do not toll the [one-year filing deadline](https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title8-section1158); calendar the I-589 deadline from the date of physical entry.

If the client cannot use CBP One because they sit outside the geographic eligibility envelope, evaluate the [Circumvention rule's rebuttal categories](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2023/05/16/2023-10146/circumvention-of-lawful-pathways) in advance and document the predicate facts contemporaneously — a later affidavit is weaker evidence than a contemporaneous medical record or law-enforcement report.
