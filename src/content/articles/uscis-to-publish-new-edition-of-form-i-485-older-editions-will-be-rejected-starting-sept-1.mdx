On September 18, 2026, USCIS will release a new edition of Form I-485 (Application to Register Permanent Residence or Adjust Status) tied to the recently finalized Public Charge Ground of Inadmissibility rule. Unlike typical form rollouts, there is no grace period: the old edition will be rejected immediately after the cutoff date.

## What changed

USCIS will publish the 09/18/26 edition of Form I-485 on September 18, 2026, replacing the 01/20/25 edition. The new form has been revised to align with [the Public Charge Ground of Inadmissibility Final Rule](https://www.federalregister.gov/public-inspection/2026-14539/public-charge-ground-of-inadmissibility).

The acceptance rules are strict and time-bound:

- **Before Sept. 18, 2026**: USCIS will accept the 01/20/25 edition if postmarked or electronically submitted.
- **On or after Sept. 18, 2026**: USCIS will reject any 01/20/25 edition and only accept the 09/18/26 edition.
- **No grace period**: Unlike some form transitions, USCIS is providing no overlap window.

USCIS is making a preview version of the 09/18/26 form and instructions available in advance, but applicants must not file the new edition before the effective date.

## Why it matters

This is a hard cutoff that will trip up practitioners and applicants if filing timing is not carefully managed. Mailing an I-485 on September 17 with the old form will be accepted; mailing the same old form on September 18 will result in a rejection notice and require resubmission with the new edition.

Because the Public Charge rule changes how USCIS evaluates receipt of government benefits, the revised form likely contains new fields or modified questions that capture the updated regulatory standard. Filing the old form after the deadline could result in an outright rejection rather than a request for amended pages—meaning lost time and the need to restart processing.

For practitioners:
- Confirm which edition your client needs based on the intended filing date.
- If your client is in the final stages of I-485 preparation and the deadline is near, prioritize using the new edition to avoid rejection.
- If you have pending I-485 applications using the old form, file them before September 18 or prepare to amend and resubmit.

## Way forward

- **Download and review the preview now.** Visit the [Form I-485 webpage](https://www.uscis.gov/i-485) to obtain the 09/18/26 edition and instructions as soon as they become available. Identify any new fields or language changes that affect how you draft your client's response.

- **Audit your filing queue.** Determine which I-485 applications are pending adjudication or still in preparation. If filing before September 18, continue using 01/20/25. If filing on or after that date, switch to 09/18/26 immediately upon release.

- **Alert clients of the hard deadline.** Communicate the no-grace-period cutoff clearly. If a client's application is ready now, do not delay filing to avoid the risk of missing the window.

- **Watch for USCIS clarification.** Monitor the [USCIS newsroom](https://www.uscis.gov/newsroom) for any technical guidance or FAQ regarding the form transition, particularly if questions arise about mailed applications in transit near the September 18 date.
