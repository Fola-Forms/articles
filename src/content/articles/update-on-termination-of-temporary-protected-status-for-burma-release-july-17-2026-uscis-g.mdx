A federal court in Illinois has ordered a postponement of the Burma TPS termination, and USCIS has released its third guidance update in 2026 to reflect an extended EAD expiration date. If you represent Burma TPS beneficiaries or process employment verifications, you need to update your records and filing instructions today.

## What changed

USCIS's July 17 guidance supersedes the July 10 message, moving the employment authorization expiration date forward by 10 days. The employment authorized through date in SAVE manual responses is now "July 27, 2026", up from July 17, 2026 in the prior update.

TPS Burma beneficiaries retain their status and employment authorization under the court order, with Forms I-766 (EADs with category A12 or C19) remaining valid and extended. The extension represents limited relief pending alignment with the U.S. Supreme Court's June 25, 2026 decision in Mullin v. Doe.

SAVE will verify TPS approval using Form I-797 or I-797C documents, displaying the manual response "Temporary Protected Status – Employment Authorized - Temp Emp Auth" with the July 27, 2026 expiration date. Status remains contingent on developments in the ongoing litigation.

## Why it matters

The repeated extensions signal that the federal court remains willing to block the DHS termination as the case proceeds. Each update changes the EAD validity date by small increments, which disrupts both employer I-9 compliance and beneficiary work planning. You must:

- **Update SAVE reference materials** if you train staff on manual verification procedures
- **Revise I-9 completion checklists** to show the correct expiration date (July 27, 2026)
- **Communicate with clients** that work authorization remains valid only if litigation favors beneficiaries
- **Monitor USCIS/E-Verify** for further guidance—the court may extend or rule against beneficiaries at any time

The gaps between updates (April 13 → July 1 → July 10 → July 17) suggest fluid litigation outcomes. Do not rely on the July 27 date as permanent.

## Way forward

- **Immediately update** your SAVE and E-Verify compliance templates to use "July 27, 2026" as the EAD expiration date.
- **On Form I-9 Section 1**, enter "as per court order"; in Section 2, list the specific document expiration plus a note referencing the court order. Attach the USCIS alert as supporting documentation.
- **Brief clients** that TPS status and work authorization hinge on the Northern District of Illinois litigation outcome. Advise them to file any dependent benefit or family-based petitions while status remains valid, to avoid gaps if the court reverses.
- **Check the [USCIS TPS Burma page](https://www.uscis.gov/humanitarian/temporary-protected-status/temporary-protected-status-designated-country-burma-myanmar) weekly** for the next guidance update.
