USCIS routes the 90-odd forms it adjudicates through three operational layers: **Lockbox** intake (mail handling and fee receipt), **Service Centers** (paper-record adjudication of petitions that do not require an interview), and **Field Offices** (interview-based adjudication of naturalization, adjustment, and certain humanitarian cases). The routing table sits in [**8 CFR §103.2**](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-8/chapter-I/subchapter-B/part-103/section-103.2), in each individual form's instructions, and in the consolidated [**USCIS Lockbox and Service Center Filing Locations**](https://www.uscis.gov/forms/filing-guidance/lockbox-and-service-center-filing-locations) page. Sending a form to the wrong layer triggers rejection or transfer — both of which add months to processing.

## What changed

USCIS overhauled the lockbox-to-service-center routing in late 2018 when it consolidated I-485 intake at the [Chicago Lockbox](https://www.uscis.gov/forms/filing-guidance/lockbox-and-service-center-filing-locations) and moved most adjustment work to the [National Benefits Center (NBC)](https://www.uscis.gov/about-us/find-a-uscis-office). The current state of play, as of the most recent [Policy Manual Vol 1, Part A](https://www.uscis.gov/policy-manual/volume-1-part-a) update:

**Lockboxes** handle intake only. They check the filing fee, scan the petition, generate an [I-797C receipt notice](https://www.uscis.gov/forms/all-forms/uscis-account-update-form-i-797c), and forward the file to the adjudicating service center or field office. Filers do not interact with the lockbox after intake. The three lockboxes are in **Chicago, IL**; **Phoenix, AZ**; and **Lewisville, TX**. Which lockbox depends on the form and the petitioner's residence — the [filing-locations page](https://www.uscis.gov/forms/filing-guidance/lockbox-and-service-center-filing-locations) is the source of truth and changes regularly.

**Service Centers** adjudicate petitions that do not require an interview. There are five:

- [**California Service Center (CSC)**](https://www.uscis.gov/about-us/find-a-uscis-office) — Laguna Niguel, CA. Heavy [I-129](https://www.uscis.gov/i-129) and [I-140](https://www.uscis.gov/i-140) workload.
- [**Nebraska Service Center (NSC)**](https://www.uscis.gov/about-us/find-a-uscis-office) — Lincoln, NE. Mixed employment and family.
- [**Texas Service Center (TSC)**](https://www.uscis.gov/about-us/find-a-uscis-office) — Dallas/Mesquite, TX. Heavy [I-130](https://www.uscis.gov/i-130) and [I-751](https://www.uscis.gov/i-751) volume.
- [**Vermont Service Center (VSC)**](https://www.uscis.gov/about-us/find-a-uscis-office) — St. Albans, VT. [VAWA I-360](https://www.uscis.gov/i-360), [U-visa I-918](https://www.uscis.gov/i-918), [T-visa I-914](https://www.uscis.gov/i-914) jurisdiction.
- [**Potomac Service Center (PSC)**](https://www.uscis.gov/about-us/find-a-uscis-office) — Arlington, VA. Heavy I-129 employment-based work.

The **National Benefits Center (NBC)** in Lee's Summit, MO performs pre-adjudication for cases that ultimately go to a field office — primarily [I-485](https://www.uscis.gov/i-485) adjustment, [I-130](https://www.uscis.gov/i-130) interviews, and certain humanitarian work.

**Field Offices** handle interview-based adjudication. The interview is mandatory for [N-400 naturalization](https://www.uscis.gov/n-400) and for most [I-485 adjustments](https://www.uscis.gov/i-485) under [8 CFR §245.6](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-8/chapter-I/subchapter-B/part-245/section-245.6) — though USCIS waives the I-485 interview for many employment-based concurrent filings under a [2018 policy update](https://www.uscis.gov/sites/default/files/document/policy-manual-updates/20210104-AdjustmentEAD.pdf). [I-751](https://www.uscis.gov/i-751) joint-petition cases are sometimes transferred from the TSC to a field office for interview when the record is mixed.

## Why it matters

Three operational consequences flow from getting the routing right.

**Wrong lockbox = rejection without prejudice.** A petition sent to the wrong lockbox is returned with the fee, unfiled. The petitioner loses the [priority date](https://travel.state.gov/content/travel/en/legal/visa-law0/visa-bulletin.html) until refiling — material on EB-2 and EB-3 cases where the [Visa Bulletin](https://travel.state.gov/content/travel/en/legal/visa-law0/visa-bulletin.html) advances quickly. The fix is mechanical: use the [filing-locations page](https://www.uscis.gov/forms/filing-guidance/lockbox-and-service-center-filing-locations) and the form's instructions on the [form page](https://www.uscis.gov/forms) and confirm the lockbox street address on the day of filing — USCIS changes lockbox routing periodically without much notice.

**Wrong service center = transfer with delay.** USCIS will sometimes transfer a misfiled petition between service centers under [8 CFR §103.4](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-8/chapter-I/subchapter-B/part-103/section-103.4) rather than reject. Transfer adds 30–90 days to processing and produces a new [I-797C transfer notice](https://www.uscis.gov/forms/all-forms/uscis-account-update-form-i-797c). The transfer does not change the receipt date — but it does interrupt premium-processing clocks under [USCIS Premium Processing](https://www.uscis.gov/forms/i-907-request-for-premium-processing). [Form I-907 premium](https://www.uscis.gov/i-907) filers should call the [Contact Center](https://www.uscis.gov/contactcenter) immediately on transfer to confirm the 15-business-day clock restarted on the right date.

**Interview venue = field-office assignment by residence.** [N-400](https://www.uscis.gov/n-400) and most [I-485](https://www.uscis.gov/i-485) interviews are assigned to the field office covering the petitioner's residence — not the petitioner's choice. The [field-office locator](https://www.uscis.gov/about-us/find-a-uscis-office) shows current assignments; some metro areas (NY, LA, Houston) have multiple field offices with different processing times. A move during pendency triggers a transfer under [Form AR-11](https://www.uscis.gov/ar-11) — required within 10 days of any change of address under [INA §265](https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title8-section1305&num=0&edition=prelim).

## Way forward

- **Check the form's filing instructions on the day of mailing.** [USCIS form pages](https://www.uscis.gov/forms) and the [filing-locations page](https://www.uscis.gov/forms/filing-guidance/lockbox-and-service-center-filing-locations) are the source of truth. Routing changes regularly; what was the right address six months ago may not be today.
- **Distinguish "form filed at" from "form adjudicated by."** The lockbox receives; the service center or field office adjudicates. Receipts show the lockbox address; the [Case Status Online tool](https://egov.uscis.gov/) shows the adjudicating office after intake completes.
- **For concurrent filings, match the [I-485](https://www.uscis.gov/i-485) lockbox to the [I-140](https://www.uscis.gov/i-140) or [I-130](https://www.uscis.gov/i-130) lockbox.** Concurrent filings go to the [I-485 lockbox](https://www.uscis.gov/forms/filing-guidance/lockbox-and-service-center-filing-locations), not the underlying-petition lockbox. The lockbox staples the bundle and forwards to the appropriate service center or NBC.
- **File AR-11 within 10 days of any move during pendency.** [INA §265](https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title8-section1305&num=0&edition=prelim) makes it a misdemeanor not to. Practically, the [online AR-11](https://egov.uscis.gov/coa/) updates the case file across pending petitions and reassigns interview venue.
- **For premium I-907 filings, mail to the [premium-processing address](https://www.uscis.gov/i-907) for the relevant service center.** Premium addresses differ from standard addresses at every service center.
- **Use [USCIS Online Account](https://my.uscis.gov/) where available.** Many forms — [I-90](https://www.uscis.gov/i-90), [I-130](https://www.uscis.gov/i-130) (under certain circumstances), [N-400](https://www.uscis.gov/n-400), [I-907](https://www.uscis.gov/i-907), [I-765](https://www.uscis.gov/i-765) (certain categories) — accept online filing, which bypasses lockbox routing entirely.
- **Track [field-office and service-center processing times separately](https://egov.uscis.gov/processing-times/)** — a [TSC I-130](https://www.uscis.gov/i-130) can move very differently from a [NSC I-130](https://www.uscis.gov/i-130), and a [Brooklyn field office N-400](https://www.uscis.gov/n-400) very differently from a [Newark field office N-400](https://www.uscis.gov/n-400).
